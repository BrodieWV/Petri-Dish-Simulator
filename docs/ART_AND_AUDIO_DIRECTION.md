# Art and Audio Direction

## Visual style

Bright, stylised, tactile, readable, and alive without looking medically disturbing.

## Rendering priorities

Strong colony silhouettes, visible growth fronts, moisture and condensation, distinct movement styles, smooth interpolation over a low-resolution simulation, and consistent overlays.

## Dish layers

Dish/medium, environmental texture, colony base, active edge, events/particles, inspection highlight, and UI overlays.

## State language

Growth advances outward; healthy metabolism has subtle motion; dry stress shrinks and cracks; excess moisture blooms or pools; heat stress reduces activity; dormancy becomes still; death breaks down gradually.

## Audio

Light ambient music with minimal melody. Feedback includes droplets, control clicks, growth chimes, discoveries, warnings, and a non-punishing collapse cue. Audio is never required to understand state.
