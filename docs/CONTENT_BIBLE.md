# Content Bible

## Tone

Curious, observational, playful, and scientifically respectful. Avoid medical horror, punishment-heavy wording, false species precision, and long textbook passages during active play.

## Organism archetypes

### Bacillus subtilis

A stylised model bacterium for exploring colony growth, resource limits, drying, and temperature response. Its simulation values are educational gameplay tuning, not strain-specific measurements or cultivation guidance.

### Filamentous Fungus

Branching threads and radial fans; teaches hyphal growth, moisture, and substrate structure.

### Slime Mould

Bright moving networks; teaches gradients, distributed behaviour, and efficient paths.

### Yeast-like Culture

Rounded budding clusters; teaches density, metabolism, and waste buildup.

## Media

Nutrient agar, low-nutrient agar, wood-chip substrate, and moist soil gel.

## Discovery categories

Growth, stress, resources, tolerance, competition, dormancy, recovery, pattern formation, contamination, and intervention.

## Discovery template

Observation; likely cause; why it matters; try next; accuracy label.
