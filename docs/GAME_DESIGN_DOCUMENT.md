# Game Design Document

## High concept

Players create a miniature microbial habitat by choosing an organism, medium, food source, and environmental conditions. The dish develops in accelerated time and produces visible colony patterns, competition, stress, dormancy, contamination, and collapse.

## Genre

Casual ecosystem sandbox, educational discovery game, and light collection simulator.

## Core loop

1. Choose dish, medium, organism, nutrients, and starting conditions.
2. Start the culture.
3. Observe colony growth and environmental feedback.
4. Intervene using temperature, moisture, light, airflow, nutrients, and inspection.
5. Reach stability, a challenge objective, dormancy, resource collapse, contamination, or death.
6. Earn discoveries, knowledge, challenge stars, cosmetics, and new content.

## Modes

- Guided Experiments: short lessons focused on one relationship.
- Challenge Cultures: restricted setups with objectives and scoring.
- Sandbox: open experimentation with unlocked content.
- Daily Dish: seeded challenge planned after MVP.

## Player feedback

Condition states include Growing, Stable, Hungry, Too Dry, Too Wet, Too Hot, Too Cold, Light Stressed, Oxygen Limited, Crowded, Dormant, and Dying.

## Failure philosophy

Failure must reveal a cause, observation, journal entry, or related experiment. Difficulty comes from interacting variables and delayed effects, not arbitrary hidden timers.

## Initial content target

Four organism archetypes, four media, five environmental variables, twelve guided experiments, twenty discoveries, ten challenges, six contamination events, and eight cosmetic unlocks.

## Out of scope for MVP

Molecular chemistry, real pathogen cultivation instructions, multiplayer, full genetics, volumetric fluid simulation, cloud accounts, and runtime language-model integration.
