# Progression and Economy

## Goals

Encourage experimentation, reward understanding, introduce complexity gradually, avoid grinding, and preserve sandbox freedom.

## Progression

Use Knowledge from discoveries and guided experiments, Culture Stars from challenge performance, and direct cosmetic unlocks. Avoid multiple soft currencies in the first release.

Early content includes rapid bacterium, nutrient agar, temperature, moisture, and journal. Mid-game adds yeast-like culture, low-nutrient agar, light, airflow, challenges, and inspection. Later adds fungus, wood chips, slime mould, contamination, and stability challenges.

## Scoring

Objectives, culture health, efficiency, interventions, completion time, stability, and optional discoveries.

## Monetisation boundaries

Preferred options are rewarded ads for optional hints or analysis, cosmetics, ad removal, and expansions. Never interrupt an active experiment, sell rescue after collapse, lock basic explanations, or impose energy systems. Monetisation is outside the first prototype.
